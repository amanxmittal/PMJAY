VISION STATEMENT 2025

Vision & Mandate

“Sustainable and Inclusive growth of Rural India through a multi-pronged strategy for eradication of poverty by increasing livelihood opportunities, providing a social safety net and developing infrastructure for growth.”

About Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin

Being the nodal Ministry for most of the development and welfare activities in the rural areas, the Ministry of Rural Development plays a pivotal role in the overall development strategy of the country. The vision and mission of the Ministry is sustainable and inclusive growth of rural India through a multipronged strategy for eradication of poverty by increasing livelihoods opportunities, providing social safety net and developing infrastructure for growth.

This is expected to improve quality of life in rural India and to correct the developmental imbalances, aiming in the process, to reach out to most disadvantaged sections of the society. In the context of housing, PMAY-G addresses housing deprivation by constructing durable pucca houses for all homeless households and households living in kutcha and dilapidated houses.

Journey So Far

Launched in November 2016, PMAY-G replaced the erstwhile Indira Awaas Yojana (IAY). Key paradigms introduced include:

  • Minimum unit size enhancement: Increased from 20 sq.m. to 25 sq.m. including a dedicated hygienic cooking space.
  • Financial grant enhancement: Raised from ₹70,000 to ₹1,20,000 in plains and ₹1,30,000 in hilly/difficult/IAP areas.
  • AwaasSoft & AwaasApp: Real-time digital workflow with geo-tagged, time-stamped photographs for every construction milestone.
  • Zero Contractor Policy: Houses are constructed solely by the beneficiaries themselves, promoting self-reliance and local employment.

Convergence Benefits for Beneficiaries

A PMAY-G house is not just a roof, but a complete habitat solution realized through seamless inter-ministerial convergence:

Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM)

Additional grant of ₹12,000 for constructing an attached sanitary latrine (toilet).

MGNREGA Wage Support

Entitlement of 90 days (plains) to 95 days (hilly areas) of unskilled labour wages directly to the beneficiary.

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

Free LPG gas connection with stove and first cylinder for smoke-free cooking.

Jal Jeevan Mission & Saubhagya

Piped drinking water tap connection and domestic electricity connection with smart LED lighting.
