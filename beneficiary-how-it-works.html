PMAY-G Registration & Construction Process

A transparent, digitally monitored process from identification to house key handover.

STEP 1

Check Eligibility

Beneficiaries are identified using the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC 2011) data based on 13 exclusion parameters and housing deprivation criteria (zero, one, or two rooms with kutcha wall and kutcha roof).

Who is prioritized?
  • Households with no adult male member aged 16 to 59
  • Female-headed households with no adult male member
  • Households with a disabled member
  • SC, ST, Minorities, and primitive tribal groups
View Full Eligibility Criteria →
STEP 2

Get Registered as Beneficiary

Eligible families are registered in the AwaasSoft national portal by the Gram Panchayat / Block Administrator. The Gram Sabha verifies the priority list and handles any claims or objections transparently.

Awaas+ 2024 Survey

Households missed in SECC 2011 are captured through the Awaas+ mobile app survey by accredited Gram Rozgar Sahayaks (GRS) and verified by District authorities.

Download AwaasApp Guide →
STEP 3

Track Your House Progress

Funds of ₹1.20 Lakh / ₹1.30 Lakh are transferred directly into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account in installments based on geotagged inspections of construction stages (Plinth, Lintel, Roof).

Milestone Verification
  • 1st Installment: Sanction & proposed site geotag
  • 2nd Installment: Plinth level completion inspection
  • 3rd Installment: Lintel & roof level completion

13 Automatic Exclusion Parameters

Under PMAY-G guidelines, households with any of the following 13 assets or conditions are automatically excluded from the beneficiary selection list:

1. Motorized Vehicles

Motorized two/three/four wheelers or motorized fishing boats.

2. Mechanized Farm Equipment

Mechanized three/four wheeler agricultural equipment or tractors.

3. Kisan Credit Card Limit

Kisan Credit Card with credit limit of ₹50,000 or above.

4. Government Employee

Any household member who is a regular government employee.

5. Non-Agricultural Enterprise

Households with government-registered non-agricultural enterprises.

6. Income Threshold

Any family member earning more than ₹15,000 per month.

7. Income Tax Payers

Households paying income tax or professional tax.

8. Refrigerator Ownership

Households owning a domestic refrigerator.

9. Landholding Criteria

Owning 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with irrigation equipment.
