QUESTION & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries regarding beneficiary eligibility, SECC list prioritization, installment transfers, geotagging, and Grievance Redressal.

Beneficiaries can visit the "I am a Beneficiary" section on this portal or use the AwaasApp mobile application. By entering their State, District, Block, Panchayat, or PMAY-G Registration ID (e.g. CH150625807), they can view real-time status of their sanction order, bank verification, geotagged inspection photos, and DBT installment payment dates.

If your household was omitted from the SECC 2011 list, the Ministry introduced the Awaas+ database to capture left-out deserving households. Gram Panchayats verify eligibility, and Gram Sabhas pass formal resolutions before submitting the updated priority list to the Block Development Officer (BDO).

PMAY-G does not require complex paperwork or application fees. Eligible families are enrolled by local Gram Rozgar Sahayaks (GRS) and Ward members during Gram Sabha meetings. You need to provide your Aadhaar card, active bank account passbook, job card (for MGNREGA wages), and consent form for geotagging.

Rural households living in kutcha houses (dilapidated thatched or mud houses with zero, one, or two rooms) or homeless families as per SECC 2011 and Awaas+ surveys. Priority is accorded to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Minorities, widows, physically challenged persons, and primitive tribal groups.

In plain areas, the total assistance is ₹1,20,000 per unit, and in hilly states/difficult areas/IAP districts, it is ₹1,30,000. It is disbursed in 3 or 4 installments:
  • 1st Installment (around ₹40,000): Upon sanction order and proposed site geotagging.
  • 2nd Installment (around ₹40,000 to ₹50,000): Upon plinth level construction verification.
  • 3rd Installment (around ₹30,000 to ₹40,000): Upon lintel/roof casting inspection.
Additionally, ₹12,000 is credited for toilet construction under SBM and 90–95 days of wage employment is paid under MGNREGA.

If the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) flags your account due to an invalid IFSC, name mismatch, or inactive Aadhaar seeding, visit your bank branch immediately to ensure NPCI Aadhaar mapping is active. Submit the updated passbook copy to your Panchayat Secretary or Block Administrator to re-validate on AwaasSoft.
