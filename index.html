Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana- Gramin

The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana Gramin (PMAY-G) is a flagship government initiative aimed at providing affordable housing to rural citizens, ensuring that families living in kutcha houses or without shelter can move into permanent, pucca homes with essential amenities.
