Housing as an instrument of poverty alleviation

The public housing programme in India provides assistance to rural families living in kutcha or dilapidated homes to construct a durable, hygienic pucca house equipped with clean tap water, sanitary toilet, and LPG connection.

HOW IT WORKS

Step-by-Step Process to Apply

Follow this clear 3-step guide to receive financial assistance, verify construction milestones, and move into your permanent pucca home.

1

Check Eligibility

Verify if your family is listed in the SECC 2011 housing deprivation list or registered in the Awaas+ database by Gram Panchayat.

Check Eligibility Criteria
2

Get Registered

Submit basic KYC, Aadhaar consent, and bank account details. Gram Sabha conducts prioritization and inspection officers geotag the proposed site.

Download AwaasApp Guide
3

Track House Progress

Receive ₹1.20 Lakh / ₹1.30 Lakh in DBT installments at Plinth, Lintel, and Roof levels. Monitor inspection photos and FTO orders directly online.

If your household was omitted from the SECC 2011 list, the Ministry conducted the Awaas+ survey to identify left-out eligible households. Gram Panchayats verify deprivation criteria and Gram Sabhas approve the final priority list.

Assistance of ₹1,20,000 per unit is provided in plain areas and ₹1,30,000 in hilly states, difficult areas, and IAP/LWE districts. In addition, beneficiaries receive 90 to 95 days of unskilled wage employment under MGNREGA and ₹12,000 for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM).

No contractor engagement is permitted under PMAY-G. Construction must be carried out by the beneficiary themselves or through local masons, ensuring community ownership and direct employment.
