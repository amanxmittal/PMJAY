About Department of Rural Development & PMAY-G

Being the nodal Ministry for most of the development and welfare activities in the rural areas, the Ministry of Rural Development plays a pivotal role in the overall development strategy of the country. The vision and mission of the Ministry is sustainable and inclusive growth of rural India through a multipronged strategy for eradication of poverty by increasing livelihoods opportunities, providing a social safety net, and developing infrastructure for growth.

This is expected to improve quality of life in rural India and to correct developmental imbalances, aiming in the process to reach out to the most disadvantaged sections of society. Under the "Housing for All" charter, Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) provides substantial financial assistance directly to beneficiary bank accounts to construct environmentally sound, disaster-resilient, and culturally appropriate permanent housing.

Journey So Far & Key Convergence Initiatives

To realize maximum socio-economic impact, PMAY-G converges with multiple flagship schemes of the Government of India:

  • Mahatma Gandhi NREGA (MGNREGA): Provision of 90/95 person-days of unskilled wage labor to each housing beneficiary.
  • Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin (SBM-G): Dedicated incentive for constructing clean, functional household sanitary latrines.
  • Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM): Functional Household Tap Connection (FHTC) providing safe drinking water.
  • Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY): Clean LPG cooking gas connection for all new houses.
  • Saubhagya / PM Surya Ghar: Guaranteed electricity connection and solar roof installations.

Program Budget & Direct Benefit Transfer

The scheme operates under a cost-sharing pattern between the Government of India and State Governments (60:40 in plain areas and 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan States; 100% UTs). Total central allocation exceeding ₹3,00,000 Crore has been directly disbursed to beneficiaries through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) and AwaasSoft, eliminating leakages and intermediaries.
